Privacy (personal data)

Data controller
Sintera Czech s.r.o., Company ID 29130336, Orlická 163/18, 500 03 Hradec Králové, Czech Republic
Enquiry and booking
Name, email, phone, dates of stay, number of guests and the text of your message — to handle your enquiry, conclude the accommodation contract and communicate with you about the stay. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
Statutory guest register
Name, date of birth and address; for foreign nationals also citizenship and ID document number — for the guest book, the tourist tax and the report to the Czech Foreign Police (UbyPort). Legal basis: legal obligation; kept for 6 years and cannot be erased on request any sooner.
Personal trip guide
A link with a token just for you. The guide knows your name, dates and party (number of adults and children, optionally interests) so it can recommend trips that fit. The link works for 14 days after departure.
Running the villa
Door and fridge code and notes about your stay (e.g. a cot, early arrival) — used only during the stay, so everything works without waiting.
Where the data is
With our processor Supabase (AWS servers in London, United Kingdom — a country with an EU adequacy decision) and in the owner’s email and calendar. Booking platforms (Booking.com, Airbnb, FeWo-direkt, e-chalupy, Megaubytko) are independent controllers under their own terms. The site and the guide are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, USA – standard contractual clauses), analytics run on our own server in Germany (Hetzner), and server backups are stored on Google Drive (Google, USA).
Retention
Contact details, notes and the door code are anonymised automatically 30 days after departure. Name, dates of stay and number of guests remain in the stay overview for 6 years. The guest register is kept for 6 years, accounting records for the period set by law.
Analytics and external sources
Traffic on the site and in the guide is measured with Umami — no cookies, no IP addresses stored (location only as country and city). Trip and weather data and the fonts come from our own sources.
Your rights
Access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz). We respond to requests within 30 days.
