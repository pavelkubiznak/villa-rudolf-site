Villa Rudolf · Who it’s for

A ski week with children

The whole house for one or more families, with the ski bus a short walk away. The car can stay parked on the grounds all week.

What you’ll appreciate

  • The SkiResort Černá hora – Pec ski bus stops 200 m from the gate and is free.
  • Slopes right in Svoboda nad Úpou 1.9 km, Černá hora and Janské Lázně 4 km, Pec pod Sněžkou 13 km — all on one ski pass.
  • A ski room in the basement: skis and snowboards have their place and boots dry overnight. Wet things stay downstairs.
  • After skiing, a Finnish sauna just for you. When it rains, the Aquacentrum indoor pool in Janské Lázně can be reached without a car.
  • No bunk beds, a free cot and two full kitchens, so cooking for the whole group is easy.
  • Shop, restaurants and rentals are 150 m from the gate.

To be honest

The pool is closed in winter. The slopes are not at the house; you get to the lifts by ski bus or car.

Price

In winter (January to mid-March) 14 900 Kč (≈ 595 €) for the whole house per night, in selected weeks in February and early January 16 400 Kč (≈ 655 €). Minimum stay 2 nights.

Other occasions

  • The whole house for your group of friends — Seven bedrooms, a sauna, billiards, a fire pit and a gazebo with grills. Nobody else past the gate.
  • A family reunion under one roof — Each family its own room and bathroom, one big table for everyone, a playground and a lockable pool.
  • Company retreats and team building — A team trip for up to 22: fast Wi-Fi, one big table, a sauna, a fire pit and outdoor programme in summer and winter.
  • A birthday party with an overnight stay — A big table, two kitchens, a gazebo with grills and a fire pit. Nobody has to drive home afterwards.
  • A summer stay with a pool and sauna — A heated, covered pool, a private sauna, a fenced 4,500 m² garden and trips on foot from the gate.
