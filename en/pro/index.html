Villa Rudolf · Who it’s for

Who Villa Rudolf is for

The whole house and its fenced grounds for one group of 6–22 people in Svoboda nad Úpou, in the Krkonoše (Giant Mountains). Depending on who you travel with:

The whole house for your group of friends

Seven bedrooms, a sauna, billiards, a fire pit and a gazebo with grills. Nobody else past the gate.

A family reunion under one roof

Each family its own room and bathroom, one big table for everyone, a playground and a lockable pool.

Company retreats and team building

A team trip for up to 22: fast Wi-Fi, one big table, a sauna, a fire pit and outdoor programme in summer and winter.

A birthday party with an overnight stay

A big table, two kitchens, a gazebo with grills and a fire pit. Nobody has to drive home afterwards.

A ski week with children

Free ski bus 200 m from the gate, slopes 1.9 km away, a ski room that dries boots and a sauna after skiing.

A summer stay with a pool and sauna

A heated, covered pool, a private sauna, a fenced 4,500 m² garden and trips on foot from the gate.
